\title[Personalized calibration for precision nutrition]{Personalized calibration transforms nutrient profiling systems for precision nutrition}

\abstract{Nutrient profiling systems provide shared food-level guidance for
population nutrition, but static scores cannot represent the person-food
heterogeneity that motivates precision nutrition. We introduce GMNPS as a
microbiome-informed demonstration of personalized calibration, in which a
bounded individual-food deviation is added to a retained population
food-quality prior. Across 9,234 foods and 15,492 microbiome profiles, the
population-mean calibrated score preserved the baseline ranking (Spearman
\(\rho=0.999839\)) and shifted only 0.68\% of foods across broad category
boundaries. The same architecture exposed bounded, model-attributed
heterogeneity with interpretable plant-matrix and lipid-related channel
attribution. Six experts reviewed the channel boundary in two rounds, supporting
the revised mask while identifying clinical translation as a future rather than
current use case. External microbiome-health analyses supported a
cardiometabolic plausibility boundary, and repeated digital-gut-twin benchmarks
showed that anchoring recovered individualized residual structure under known
assumptions while preserving the population prior. These findings support
personalized calibration as a feasible route for precision-ready nutrient
profiling, with prospective or deeply phenotyped retrospective validation still
required before clinical use.}

\keywords{nutrient profiling; precision nutrition; gut microbiome; personalized calibration; digital gut twin}
