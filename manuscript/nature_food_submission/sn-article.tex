\title[Bounded calibration for nutrient profiling]{Bounded attribute-level calibration for microbiome-informed nutrient profiling}

\abstract{Nutrient-profile models rate the nutritional quality of individual foods for defined public-health applications. Postprandial glycaemic responses to the same foods can differ substantially between individuals. The Gut Microbiome-informed Nutrient Profiling System (GMNPS) uses bounded attribute-level calibration with locked inputs. Eligible observed participant-by-meal outcomes are unavailable in the audited data, limiting the current evidence tier to computational feasibility. The locked implementation recovered the programmed mapping in a correctly specified synthetic positive-control. The framework provides an auditable basis for future empirical testing, but GMNPS does not establish external validity.}

\keywords{nutrient profiling; personalized calibration; gut microbiome; computational feasibility; synthetic positive-control}
