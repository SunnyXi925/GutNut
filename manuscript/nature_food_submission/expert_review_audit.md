## Audited finding

The repository C1 and C3 workbooks are blank review instruments. The repository
E4 document contains no completed checkboxes or comments. They do not record a
completed review outcome.

Three external C1 round-two feedback workbooks are available. Across 90
nutrient-assignment judgements, the H-column assignment decisions comprise 86
`合理`, one `需补充说明`, and three `不合理`. The I-column evidence ratings comprise
82 `充分`, seven `不充分`, and one `需补充说明`. The hash-bound anonymous item
records are in `expert_review_item_audit.csv`; its `workbook_id` values reveal
neither filenames nor reviewer identities. The available comments support the
documented revisions to carbohydrate, zinc, copper, and vitamin A RAE nutrient
channels.

## Scientific boundary

The available records do not establish an exact expert denominator, unanimous
final-round passage, a C3 agreement percentage, a count of C3 clarification
requests, or completed E4 verdicts. Anonymous item-level C3 and E4 records are
`AUTHOR_INPUT_NEEDED`.

The only authorized manuscript interpretation is qualitative: available expert
feedback informed nutrient-channel revisions and supports content review. It
does not establish outcome validity, external validity, clinical readiness,
biological superiority, or superiority of an expert-revised mask.
